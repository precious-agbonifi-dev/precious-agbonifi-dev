# Designing Business Continuity Plans for Cloud Environments

> **Author:** Precious Agbonifi | PMP · PRINCE2 · AWS Certified

## Introduction

Business continuity planning has been fundamentally transformed by the 
cloud — yet many organisations are still applying on-premise BCP thinking 
to cloud infrastructure and wondering why their recovery tests keep 
failing. Having designed and stress-tested BCP and disaster recovery 
frameworks across public sector organisations including the Greater London 
Authority, The British Library, and MOPAC, I've seen the consequences of 
treating cloud resilience as an afterthought: missed RTO targets, 
untested runbooks, and leadership teams discovering mid-incident that 
their "backed up" data hasn't been restoration-tested in eighteen months. 
Cloud environments offer extraordinary resilience capabilities — 
multi-region failover, automated backups, infrastructure-as-code 
rebuilds — but only if they are deliberately designed, regularly tested, 
and owned by someone accountable. This guide is a practitioner's 
reference for building BCP and DR strategies that actually work when the 
moment arrives.
