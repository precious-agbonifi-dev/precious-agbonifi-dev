# AI Governance: Managing Risk in Enterprise Artificial Intelligence

> **Author:** Precious Agbonifi | PMP · PRINCE2 · AWS Certified

## Introduction

Artificial intelligence is moving faster than the governance frameworks 
designed to oversee it — and organisations that deploy AI without 
structured risk management are accumulating liabilities they don't yet 
fully understand. Bias in model outputs, opaque decision-making, 
inadequate audit trails, and regulatory non-compliance are not 
hypothetical risks; they are active, documented failures happening across 
industries right now. Having worked at the intersection of technology 
delivery, GRC, and IT risk management across public sector bodies 
including MOPAC, the Greater London Authority, and Queen Mary University 
of London, I understand how governance frameworks need to evolve to 
accommodate AI — not by slowing innovation down, but by giving 
organisations the confidence to move forward responsibly. This guide 
provides a practical AI governance framework: how to assess AI risk, 
structure oversight, detect and mitigate bias, maintain audit 
trails, and align with emerging regulatory requirements including the EU 
AI Act and the UK's AI governance principles.
