# IT SOX Controls: A Practical Implementation Guide

> **Author:** Precious Agbonifi | PMP · PRINCE2 · AWS Certified

## Introduction

IT SOX (Sarbanes-Oxley Act Section 404) compliance is one of the most 
misunderstood areas in enterprise technology — often treated as a 
tick-box audit exercise rather than what it truly is: a framework for 
building trustworthy, well-governed IT systems. Having implemented and 
evidenced IT general controls across organisations including MOPAC, the 
Greater London Authority, The British Library, and Queen Mary University 
of London, I've seen first-hand how the difference between passing and 
failing an audit rarely comes down to whether controls exist — it comes 
down to whether they are consistently operated, properly evidenced, and 
genuinely understood by the people running them. This guide distils that 
experience into a practical, no-jargon reference for IT professionals, 
risk managers, and technology leaders who need to design, implement, or 
strengthen IT SOX controls in their organisation.
